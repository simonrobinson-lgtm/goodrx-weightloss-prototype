How GLP-1s help you lose weight

GLP-1s, GIPs and related medications mimic hormones your body already makes. Learn how they work in different parts of your body.

Gut
In your gut

Your gut sends a signal

GLP-1s mimic certain hormones your gut releases after eating. The medication holds that signal steady instead of letting it fade in minutes.

In your stomach

Digestion slows down

Food passes more slowly through your digestive tract, helping you to feel fuller longer.

In your brain

Food noise gets quieter

The medication targets areas of your brain that regulate appetite and food cravings. Constant thoughts about food fade.
