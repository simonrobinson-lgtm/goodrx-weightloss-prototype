Common side effects

Mild nausea & constipation

Your digestion slows down, so you might experience some discomfort.

Manage side effects
  • Eat smaller meals, throughout the day
  • Have your last meal 3 hours before bedtime
  • Stay hydrated

Light headaches

Eating less and shifts in hydration can bring them on early in treatment.

Manage side effects
  • Sip water steadily through the day
  • Try not to skip meals
  • Rest somewhere quiet and dim
Everyday Life

Treatment affects more than digestion. Your energy, habits and social plans may shift too.

Energy dips

Eating less can leave you running low, especially in the afternoon.

  • Add protein to every meal
  • Sip water throughout the day

Eating out

Smaller portions can make restaurant meals feel like a lot.

  • Order a starter as your main
  • Pack up half before you begin

Timing and results vary from person to person. Think of this as a guide, not a forecast.
